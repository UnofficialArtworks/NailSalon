# Nail Salon: competitor research and next-release discussion

## Approved creative activities implemented

Chris approved brush-through stencils, two-color marble dipping and inspiration cards available earlier. The Polish tray now includes heart/star/flower masks with tap filling and lifting, plus an optional bowl with two unlocked colors, four repeatable swirl variations and a dip action. These follow the creation mechanics described by [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/girls-nail-salon---nail-games), using original geometry and local artwork. Free-play inspiration is available from the start, with suggestions filtered to unlocked supplies, supporting optional creativity described by [Budge](https://budgestudios.com/en/apps/detail/hello-kitty-nail-salon/). All activities preserve forgiving scoring, undo, browser saves and PNG export. No advertisements, deadlines or consumption were added.

## Follow-up review: photo customization

Revisited the original findings and the official Poki, CrazyGames and Budge descriptions on October 2, 2026. Photo finishing is still a strong fit: [Nails DIY](https://poki.com/en/g/nails-diy-manicure-master) explicitly ends with rings and a photo shoot. The expanded local studio adds backdrop/frame/prop choices and jewelry, with a mix of immediate access and existing star milestone rewards. All original photo choices remain free. Shared foreground SVG artwork appears in both gallery previews and PNG export, and old photo saves default to no frame or props.

Most of the earlier studio recommendations have now shipped: stages, lengths, materials, supply collections, copying, preparation feedback, occasion cards/reactions, scrapbook names/favorites and creative prompts. Remaining opportunities, in recommended order:

1. **Brush-through stencils.** [CrazyGames' Nail Salon](https://www.crazygames.com/game/girls-nail-salon---nail-games) describes stencil painting. An optional heart/star/flower mask could let a child brush freely and then lift the stencil for a clean result. Keep tap-to-fill, undo, and normal painting available. This would add a creative action distinct from our current one-tap patterns.
2. **Two-color marble polish.** The same official description includes marble dipping. A small deterministic swirl tool could provide a satisfying reveal and reproducible export. Prototype the interaction and measure it on the actual iPad before expanding it.
3. **Picture-led inspiration accessible before all unlocks.** Budge's [Hello Kitty Nail Salon](https://budgestudios.com/en/apps/detail/hello-kitty-nail-salon/) combines unique designs with challenges. We already offer optional prompts after all milestones; a separate small starter inspiration deck could help children who do not know what to create, using only their available supplies and no grading.

These are recommendations, not additional implemented features. Do not add monetization, competitive ratings or streaks. The most valuable validation still missing is a short child playtest and physical iPad Safari testing.

Research date: October 2, 2026. Status: research complete. Chris selected **playful creative studio** as the direction. The original findings below record the initial proposals; the follow-up above distinguishes shipped features from remaining recommendations.

## Main finding

The best opportunity is to make the existing creative tools feel delightful and coherent before expanding the number of activities. Our game already has free play, customer wishes, stars, unlocks, shapes, painting, patterns, stickers, gems, room choices, a gallery, and export. What could distinguish the next release is an inviting workspace, attractive materials, easier composition across five nails, and a memorable finished-design presentation.

This is a design judgment from the comparison below, not a measured result from child testing. Test the proposed direction with Chris's daughter before expanding it.

## Method and limits

- Reviewed seven relevant products through official App Store listings, developer pages, and the official Poki/CrazyGames portals. Six are games; YouCam Nails is an adjacent design tool.
- Selected large established mobile titles, examples designed for children, and browser competitors. There is no verified global ranking across these platforms. Ratings and vote counts are platform-specific signals, not comparable measures of design quality.
- Visually inspected official Hello Kitty, Pazu, and Acrylic Nails screenshots, the Nails DIY browser loading presentation, and our live game. This is not a hands-on review of all seven games. Nails DIY remained at its loading/presentation screen during inspection; its later gameplay details come from its official description.
- Read selected published player reviews for qualitative friction. Several reviews are years old; they suggest hypotheses to test rather than describing every player's experience or proving current-version defects.
- Queried the existing local Graphify map, then inspected current types, rendering, preparation, and gallery source to assess feasibility. Graph results identify relationships; direct source inspection establishes current behavior.
- Did not download native apps, purchase subscriptions, copy third-party assets, or modify/deploy the game.

## What the comparison suggests

### 1. Make the nail and tools the visual focus

Our desktop view currently leaves substantial decorative space around a relatively small hand. It has a good foundation of pictured tool tabs, but the artwork should dominate the space the player uses. Show a prominent selected nail, a compact five-nail navigator, and one active supply tray. Keep the request pictures visible in customer mode. On iPad portrait, controls should be reachable without repeatedly scrolling between the nail and the tray.

Use pictured stages — Prepare, Paint, Decorate, Reveal — as navigation, not mandatory gates. Children must be able to go back freely. Preparation still contains nail shapes. The fixed round finger silhouette and the nail's small free edge remain deliberate invariants.

This builds on the large-nail composition seen in Hello Kitty's official screenshot, but should keep our own layout, illustrations, and language.

### 2. Improve material rendering before adding many colors

Suggested first finishes: glossy, glitter, and pearlescent. Matte and metallic can follow if the first set reads clearly on a small screen. A finish picker should change a preview immediately and preserve the selected color. Existing flat colors remain useful; finishes should create visibly different results rather than merely change labels.

Improve gem variety as well: our twelve gem supplies currently share one faceted geometry in the renderer. Give a small initial set distinct silhouettes, highlights, and scale. Add stronger contrast to stickers so they remain recognizable in the hand overview.

Static sparkle textures should be seeded from stored design data or stable identifiers. Animation belongs to presentation. PNG export must reproduce the same material, pattern, and decoration result without depending on animation time.

### 3. Make decorating a five-nail set easier

Add copy-to-selected-nails with a picture preview and one undo action. Distinguish copying base color from copying the complete design. Retain per-nail editing. Add duplicate decoration, bring forward/send backward, and easy deselection. Keep the existing button-based movement and rotation available.

Group supplies into original kits such as Candy Pop, Ocean Sparkle, Garden Party, and Rainbow Dreams. Start with a few well-made kits using existing supplies. A kit helps discovery but must not restrict mixing materials. Show actual pattern previews on a nail, and add a pattern color picker; current patterns largely use fixed colors.

These changes reduce repeated work without taking away drawing. Full-copy operations need fresh decoration IDs and independent stroke/decoration data so editing one nail never changes another.

### 4. Make preparation satisfying but brief

Replace the instant-looking wipe with visible dirt removal, soft bubbles, a sponge following the pointer, and a short clean sparkle. Add a file interaction that previews the chosen nail shape. Both activities need tap alternatives and a skip in free play; cancellation or rotation must not lose progress.

Prototype one tactile interaction first. Do not add a long mandatory sequence for each of five nails. The objective is enjoyment, not simulated salon accuracy or repetitive chores.

### 5. Give customers context without making requests stricter

Add an illustrated occasion card: a rainbow party, beach trip, garden picnic, or space celebration. Preserve the existing two objective wishes, color and sticker, for scoring. The theme inspires the player but introduces no hidden grading. Let customers briefly wave on arrival and show a friendly reaction when finished.

Keep themes available whenever the player wants them. No daily deadlines or streak penalties. Returning favorites could remember a previous gallery picture later, but that requires explicit saved history and should be a separate decision.

### 6. Turn finishing into a photo-studio moment

Offer a small selection of locally bundled backdrops, an optional ring/bracelet, a clear save action, and a replayable reveal. The gallery can resemble a scrapbook with editable names and favorites. After a customer, show stars individually and preview the next unlocked kit. After all milestones, optional collection cards can offer creative prompts without making more materials inaccessible.

The existing gallery and PNG export are foundations; the proposed improvement is presentation and customization, not adding saving for the first time. Preserve the explicit 50-design limit and deletion choice. If hand jewelry is included, on-screen and exported geometry must share anchors to avoid another bracelet alignment problem.

## Player-review lessons

Selected Hello Kitty reviews complain about paid access interrupting continued decorating; Acrylic Nails reviews describe mid-manicure advertisements and unappealing animation. Together with the YouCam discovery/copy requests, these support keeping sessions uninterrupted, making supplies easy to locate, and reducing repeated composition work. They do not justify introducing purchases, advertisements, accounts, public voting, or a social feed. [Hello Kitty reviews](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/hello-kitty-nail-salon/id949737354), [Acrylic Nails reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/acrylic-nails/id1526638009), [YouCam reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/youcam-nails-nail-art-salon/id1051710880?platform=vision).

## Architectural fit

Graph query expanded using existing vocabulary: manicure, prep, tool, render, pattern, decoration, customer, reward, gallery, save, tutorial, room. The traversal returned 149 nodes with a 102-node budgeted excerpt. Recommendations do not assume unseen edges.

Relevant graph sources: `renderNail()` in `src/art/render.ts`, `Nail` and `Manicure` in `src/game/types.ts`, `useEditor()` in `src/editor/useEditor.ts`, `ManicurePrep()` in `src/components/ManicurePrep.tsx`, `Gallery()` in `src/components/Gallery.tsx`, and validation/save nodes. Current source inspection corroborates their responsibilities.

- Keep nail artwork declarative and normalized. Extend rendering for materials and editable pattern parameters; avoid storing screenshots as the editable source.
- Keep copy, duplicate, pattern, and finish edits in the editor/history layer. Keep transient tool animations out of persistent artwork and undo snapshots.
- Keep occasion descriptions separate from the forgiving customer score rules.
- Put stage navigation and photo presentation in focused components/hooks; avoid adding every feature directly to App.
- Any new persisted fields require explicit compatible defaults or a version migration, validation, recovery, and old-save tests. Migration must preserve the active manicure, gallery, and earned supplies.
- Do not assume Canvas animation will meet the iPad target. Use cached/static material textures and bounded effects, then measure on the actual device.

Release each slice after review instead of combining everything into one large redesign. Keep Chromium, Firefox, WebKit, subdirectory, and iframe verification. Physical iPad Safari painting and a short child playtest remain essential.

## Approved first milestone

Chosen emphasis: **playful creative studio**. Satisfying preparation and friendly customer progression support the creative experience.

Recommended first milestone: slices A–C (workspace, materials/collections, composition shortcuts). Start with Candy Pop, Ocean Sparkle, and Garden Party, using original art. Use the bright, glossy arcade reference images Chris supplied as the visual direction. Prototype one finished nail and one complete five-nail set before expanding the asset library. Customer and photo presentation can follow as a second milestone.

Chris approved moving forward with the playful creative studio and including short/medium/long nail lengths. The first milestone implements pictured stages and actual-art navigation, glossy/glitter/pearlescent finishes, the three collection filters, editable pattern ink, distinct gem silhouettes, matching-set copying and decoration duplication/layers. Length changes only the nail artwork bounds; the finger silhouette stays fixed. Shared geometry serves the hand and PNG export, and normalized artwork survives resizing.

Preparation feedback and customer/photo presentation remain follow-up slices. Pedicures, 3D conversion, camera try-on and an expanded furniture economy remain separate scope decisions. Physical iPad Safari and a child playtest remain unverified until tested on the actual device.
